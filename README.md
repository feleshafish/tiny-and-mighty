Tiny the leprechaun is trying to get his money and funny up by filling up his bag of gold! He somehow found himself in a cloud realm and doesn't know how to get past the spikes.

He was about to give up until.. The MIGHTY HAND appeared to help this little leprechaun.
With the help of Tiny and Mighty, go on a short, fun adventure as they collect coins!
